About Al Aman

Our Mission

Our Team

Our team is comprised of highly trained and experienced professionals who are dedicated to delivering exceptional service. With a deep understanding of the occupational health industry, we are able to provide customized solutions to meet the unique needs of our clients.

Our Commitment

Occupational Health Services

Pre-Employment Health Checks

Our pre-employment health checks are designed to ensure that potential employees are physically and mentally fit for the job they are applying for. We work with employers to establish specific requirements and tailor our assessments accordingly.

On-Site Occupational Health Services

We provide on-site occupational health services to businesses of all sizes. Our team of experienced professionals can offer a range of services, from health and safety assessments to employee wellness programs.

Drug and Alcohol Screening

Our drug and alcohol screening services help employers to maintain a safe and healthy workplace. Our testing methods are accurate and efficient, and our team is trained to handle sensitive situations with discretion and professionalism.

Health and Safety Consultancy

Our health and safety consultancy services are designed to help businesses comply with relevant legislation and regulations. We can provide risk assessments, training, and ongoing support to ensure that your workplace is safe for all employees.

Stress Management and Counselling

We offer stress management and counselling services to help employees cope with the demands of their job. Our team of trained professionals can provide one-on-one support, group sessions, and ongoing guidance as needed.

Health and Wellness Programs

Our health and wellness programs are designed to promote healthy habits and lifestyles among employees. We offer a range of services, from nutrition counselling to fitness classes, to help your workforce stay healthy and productive.
